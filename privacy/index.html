In short: Work&Pay stores everything on your iPhone. I, the developer, never receive your jobs, shifts or pay. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it shares no data with anyone. It contains no code from other companies for analytics or advertising.

Who is responsible

Work&Pay is made by {{NAME}}, {{ADDRESS}}, Denmark. Email: {{EMAIL}}.

I am the data controller for the personal data described under “When you email me”.

Data in the app

Everything you enter (jobs, pay rates, tax settings, shifts, notes and your monthly goal) is stored only on your iPhone. The app does not send it to me or to anyone else, so I cannot see, change or delete it. You are in full control of it.

  • Calendar. Only if you choose to import shifts from your calendar, the app asks for access to your calendars. It reads events from the calendars you select, on your iPhone, and saves only the start and end times of the shifts you choose to import. You can remove the access at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Work&Pay.
  • Photos. If you import shifts from a screenshot, you pick one picture, and the app only gets access to that picture. Its text is read on your iPhone with Apple’s built-in text recognition. The picture is not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Backup file. When you tap “Gem backup”, the app creates a file with all your data, and you choose where to send or save it. The file is not encrypted, so only send it to yourself.
  • Device backups. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, that backup includes the app’s data. Apple or your computer handles it according to your own settings.

Deleting your data

The data stays on your iPhone until you delete it. Tap “Slet alle data” under the profile icon in the app, or delete the app. Copies in device backups or in backup files you have saved disappear when you delete those backups or files.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. I do not receive your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. The app checks on your iPhone which purchases you have made, so that what you bought is unlocked. Apple sends me sales reports that do not show who you are. Apple’s privacy policy covers the payment itself.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have turned on Share With App Developers (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with me. Apple only shares them in a form that does not identify you. I use them only to find and fix errors. You can turn this off at any time.

If you email me, I receive your email address, your name if you include it, and what you write. I use it only to answer you.

  • Legal basis: my legitimate interest in answering questions about the app (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete the personal data I hold about you, to restrict how I use it and, where it applies, to receive it in a format you can take elsewhere. Email me at {{EMAIL}}.

For the data in the app, you don’t need to ask me: you can see, change and delete everything yourself in the app.

Your right to object: You can object at any time, on grounds relating to your situation, to my use of your emails. I will then delete them unless I have compelling legitimate grounds or need them to handle a legal claim.

You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, Denmark, dt@datatilsynet.dk, www.datatilsynet.dk, or to the data protection authority in the EU country where you live.

I make no automated decisions about you and do no profiling. I do not sell or share personal data.

Changes

The date at the top shows when this policy last changed. If I change it in a way that matters to you, for example if the app starts syncing with iCloud, I will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
