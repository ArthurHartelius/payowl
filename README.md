# Work&Pay

The website for Work&Pay, an iPhone app for keeping track of shifts, supplements and pay after tax for hourly jobs in Denmark.

Every page exists in English and in Danish (under `/da/`). The DA/EN switch at the top right goes to the same page in the other language.

| Page | English | Danish | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | `/` | `/da/` | Marketing URL in App Store Connect |
| Privacy Policy | `/privacy/` | `/da/privatliv/` | Privacy Policy URL in App Store Connect (per language). The Danish page is the link inside the app |
| Support | `/support/` | `/da/support/` | Support URL in App Store Connect (per language) |

## Rules for this site

- No cookies, analytics, web fonts, embedded videos or files from other websites. Everything is plain HTML, `style.css` and the images in `assets/`.
- The English and Danish pages must always say the same thing. Change both and update the date at the top of both policies.
- Update the policy before releasing an app version that changes what happens to data, for example iCloud sync, a server, analytics or a new permission. Tell users about important changes in the app.
- Keep the app's privacy answers in App Store Connect in line with this policy.
